02 · First launch

It isn’t broken. It’s Gatekeeper.

NetSpeed is signed ad-hoc, not with a paid Apple developer certificate, so macOS blocks the first launch on every Mac that downloads it. You only do this once.

  1. 1 Drag it into Applications Open {{DMG}} and drag NetSpeed onto the Applications folder.
  2. 2 Launch it — and let it fail macOS says it can’t verify the developer. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. 3 Press Open Anyway Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the note about NetSpeed and press Open Anyway. Confirm with your password.
Prefer the terminal? Clear the download flag instead of steps 2–3:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NetSpeed.app

After that there’s no Dock icon and no window — look for the four bars in the menu bar. To start it with your Mac, turn on Settings → Launch at login.

03 · In the menu bar

Four bars that mean something.

The bars go green, amber or red by how the connection actually behaves — not by signal strength. On a hotspot, the phone’s own cellular type sits next to them.

Native Swift, no dependencies, about 0.5% of one core at idle.
